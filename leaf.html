Leaf AI

Meet Leaf, your research companion.

Leaf is Mintly's AI learning partner. It reads with you, shows its evidence and never says buy or sell. Leaf helps you think a decision through, and the decision is always yours.

LLeafReading 2 articles about NVIDIA
Summarize this article.
Big cloud companies are spending record amounts on data centers, and NVIDIA supplies most of the chips. Analysts expect strong sales to continue for several quarters.
What's the strongest argument against it?
Customers are spending far more than AI products currently earn. If that spending slows, growth could drop quickly, and the stock price already expects a lot.
Are these articles actually saying different things?
Partly. One says demand is outrunning supply, the other says the spending may be a bubble. Both agree sales are strong. They disagree on whether it lasts.
What Leaf does

Everything you need to read, question and decide.

Summarize

Add articles to Leaf and ask what they say. Leaf pulls out the key facts, the evidence supporting the company and the reasons for caution.

Challenge

Ask for the strongest case for, or against. Leaf makes the argument its supporters or critics would, then asks which risk would hurt most.

Compare

Leaf shows where your sources agree and where they disagree, and nudges you to find one that disagrees with you.

Flag what's missing

Leaf points out what you haven't checked yet: how the price compares with earnings, who the biggest customers are, and what would change your mind.

Help with your writing

Leaf suggests edits right in your argument: tighten your wording, add evidence from your research, check for missing risks or help you organize. You decide what to accept.

Check the price

Ask what a stock is trading at and Leaf shows the latest quote, then reminds you that a price alone doesn't tell you whether it's cheap or expensive.

How Leaf teaches

Questions, not answers.

Leaf uses Socratic tutoring: it answers each question with another question. It helps you think it through and never decides for you, so the reasoning is your own.

1Research what people are saying
2Understand the evidence on both sides
3Question it before you trust it
4Decide, and name the biggest risk

Leaf's ground rules

Built to help you think, not to think for you.

Leaf never tells you to buy or sell. It shows where its information comes from, keeps the argument in your own words, and works with practice money only.

✓Never says buy or sell
✓Shows its evidence
✓Your words carry the argument
✓No real money involved
Mintly doesn't teach young people what to think about money. It teaches them how to think when making financial decisions.
Leaf's examples use mock data and are not financial advice.
