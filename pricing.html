Good to know

Is any real money invested?

No. Every simulation uses play money so kids can practice and make mistakes safely.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — paid plans can be cancelled at any time and you'll keep access until the end of your billing period.

Is this a real charge right now?

Not yet — Mintly's billing isn't live. Choosing a plan creates a demo account so you can preview the subscription experience today.
