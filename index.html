How it works

Five steps from curiosity to a decision.

  1. 1Choose

    Pick something you're curious about.

  2. 2Research

  3. 3Ask Leaf

  4. 4Make your case

    Would you buy it? Say why, and name the biggest risk.

  5. 5Simulate and reflect

    See how it plays out and get your Decision Score.
