Why Mintly

Kids meet money long before anyone teaches them about it.

Most people learn about money the hard way: as adults, after the expensive mistakes. Mintly gives teens a place to build that understanding early, and to practice real money decisions before real money is on the line.

Understand the basics

Saving, budgeting, interest, risk and investing, explained in simple language.

Practice safely

Make a decision with simulated money and see how it could play out over time.

Think it through with Leaf

Leaf reads with you, shows its evidence and answers questions with questions. It never says buy or sell.

How Mintly works

From curiosity to a decision.

Pick something you're curious about, from sneakers to trading cards to a stock everyone's talking about. Then work through it the way a careful investor would.

For parents
