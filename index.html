    Mintly's long-term ambition is to become the place where young people learn how to do things with money, and are supported in putting that knowledge into practice.

    Real financial activity would be introduced only through appropriately structured partnerships, safeguards and parental involvement.

    Mintly doesn't teach young people what to think about money. It teaches them how to think when making financial decisions.
