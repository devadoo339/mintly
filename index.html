About Mintly

Money skills, taught the way kids actually learn

Mintly exists because most people learn about money the hard way — as adults, after the expensive mistakes. We wanted a place for teens to build that understanding early, without any real money on the line.

Learn by doing

Financial literacy that feels like an app, not a textbook.

Understand the basics

Saving, budgeting, interest, risk, diversification, and investing explained in simple language.

Practice safely

Use simulated portfolios and guided scenarios to see how choices can play out over time.

Build confident habits

Set goals, complete money missions, and track progress with positive, age-appropriate feedback.

Purpose

Don't just learn about money. Learn how to use it.

Teens practice real money decisions before real money is on the line. Short lessons, then five steps: choose a topic, research it, question it, make your case, and reflect.

The Mintly experience

Five steps you can try right here.

Pick something you're curious about, then work through it the way a careful investor would.

  1. 1

    Choose

    Pick what you're curious about: sneakers, trading cards, crypto and more.

  2. 2

    Research

    Dig into why it has value, who is buying, and what could go wrong.

  3. 3

    Ask Leaf

    Question it with Leaf, who answers your questions with better questions.

  4. 4

    Make your case

    Would you buy it? Write your reason and name the biggest risk.

  5. 5

    Simulate and reflect

    Watch a practice portfolio with no real money, then reflect on what surprised you.
